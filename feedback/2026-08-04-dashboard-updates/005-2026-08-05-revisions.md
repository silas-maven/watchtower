# Owner Update 005 — 5 August revisions

**Captured:** 5 August 2026
**Source:** Kyser, WhatsApp, two screenshots. One is the live Personal Finance page showing the new `MEMBERS` badge on Pension Drawdown; the other is a reference marking a condensed set of market cards above a full-width `View more` bar.

This round amends two decisions taken on 4 August. It is recorded here rather than by editing `002` so the reversal is visible next to the original.

## 1. Pension Drawdown becomes free

**Amends `002`, "Access and commercial rule", which specified a freemium gate.**

- The calculator is available to every signed-in member. No membership required.
- Implemented by removing the route's `layout.tsx` `MemberGate` and setting `free: true` in `lib/portfolioTools.ts`. That flag is what drives the `MEMBERS` badge, the locked state on the Portfolio tools index and the locked state on the Dashboard toolkit, so all four surfaces follow from the one change.

The commercial reading, for the record: the calculator is pure client-side arithmetic with no API call and no AI spend, so giving it away costs nothing to serve. What is paid remains the academy's call on assets.

## 2. Condense the Dashboard market cards

**Extends `002` section 1, which moved the news preview up but left the tile count alone.**

- The Dashboard shows **six** market cards, not ten.
- A full-width **View more** action below them opens Market Pulse.

Six is taken from the owner's own marking on `004-market-dashboard-placement.jpg`: his blue line sits directly under SILVER and BOE RATE, tiles five and six of the flat order. The condensed set is therefore everything above that line. It drops UK 10Y GILT and ITRAXX 5Y, both of which render `—` on that same screenshot because neither has a live feed, plus VIX and DXY.

Consequences accepted:

- The inline **View full market dashboard** expander is gone from the Dashboard. The destination is now a page, so an expander alongside a link would be two routes to the same content.
- Market Pulse opens with every row already showing, since it is where **View more** lands.
- The three-article news preview keeps its 4 August placement. It sits under the tiles, which is the same position as the owner's line now that the condensed set ends there.
- The news preview no longer carries its own **View more**. The panel's link is directly beneath it and points at the same page; two identical actions a few pixels apart is worse than one.

## Not changed

The portfolio chart visible in the 5 August reference image is a mockup, not a live surface. `002` already records that Kyser wants chart-style portfolio metrics; that remains outstanding and was not part of this round's instruction.
